من الإصدار ١٫١، يرسل التطبيق أحداث استخدام إلى خدمتنا على apps.hossamhamouda.com، ويتحكم فيها مفتاح «مشاركة بيانات استخدام مجهولة» في الإعدادات، وهو مفعّل افتراضيًا. تساعدنا هذه الأحداث على فهم ما ينجح في التطبيق وما يحتاج إلى تحسين، مثل بدء تدريب أو إنهائه أو فتح شاشة الشراء.

للتواصل

لأي سؤال عن الخصوصية، راسلنا على privacy@hossamhamouda.com.

No ads, no account, and we never ask for your child's name or any personal detail. From version 1.1 the app sends anonymous usage steps that help us improve it; you can switch them off at any time in Settings.

What stays on your device

The app saves your child's progress — which tables they have mastered, how long they practised, and which facts are still shaky — in a single file on the device itself. That file never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous usage sharing (optional)

From version 1.1 the app sends usage events to our own service at apps.hossamhamouda.com. The "Share anonymous usage data" switch in Settings controls this, and it is on by default. The events help us understand what works in the app and what needs improving — for example, starting or finishing a drill, or opening the plans screen.

An event can include a random identifier for this installation, the app and iOS versions, the device type, the language, the App Store country and the step itself (such as the table number, how many answers were right, and how long it took). It never includes a name, an email address or anything anyone typed. The random identifier groups the events of one installation, so this is not "no data" — but it does not tell us who your child is.

If you buy Mizo Full while sharing is on, the app passes the random identifier to Apple with the purchase, so Apple's notifications about the purchase, price, country and any refund reach us linked to that identifier. We never receive your Apple Account or card details. Turning sharing off stops future events and deletes the identifier and the event queue from the device, but Apple can still send notifications about a purchase that was already linked to it.

What we do not do

  • We show no advertising, work with no ad network and use no advertising identifier.
  • We use no third-party analytics or tracking tools; the usage service above is our own.
  • We never ask for a name, email address, phone number or any personal detail.
  • We request no access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • We sell no data and share it with no one.

Your purchase

Mizo Full is a single payment handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card number or your account details; Apple's system only tells the app whether Mizo Full is unlocked. Payments are covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Spoken audio

The app uses the speech engine built into iOS to read questions aloud. The system handles this on the device, and we send no audio or text to anyone.

Children

Children use this app, which is why it has no advertising, asks no one for a name or any personal information, and keeps its usage events anonymous and easy to switch off.

Retention and your rights

Usage and purchase records are kept in our service for 180 days and then deleted. Turning sharing off does not immediately delete what we already received. You can ask to see or delete your data by writing to us; if a random identifier can no longer be matched to you, we will explain that.

Changes to this policy

If any of the above changes, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
